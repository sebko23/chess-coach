## 0. The problem at hand

The v1 narrative-gold corpus (`tests/gold/narrative/v1/corpus.json`, 5 placeholder entries) was a stub. The plan was to hand-curate a v1.human corpus from chess experts, but the work was open-ended and unscheduled. The v2 audit found that:

- **Phase 3 (grounded narration) was blocked** on the v1 human-curation gap.
- **Phase 4 (profile metric explanation) was blocked** on the same gap.

The honest options were:
1. Wait for hand-curation (could be months).
2. Replace with a deterministic auto-derived corpus that has a mechanical contract.

BBF-87 chose option 2.

## 1. What ships

The v2 corpus (`tests/gold/narrative/v2/corpus.json`, 30 entries: 12 opening + 12 middlegame + 6 endgame; sourced from 12 lichess_game + 18 book attributions).

### 1.1 The auto-curator

`scripts/curate_narrative_gold_auto.py` (NEW, 753 LOC): auto-curator that picks positions from real Lichess PGN exports, balances opening/middlegame/endgame, optionally enriches with lichess cloud-eval API results.

### 1.2 The corpus content

30 entries:
- 12 opening positions
- 12 middlegame positions
- 6 endgame positions

Sources:
- 12 lichess_game (real user PGNs)
- 18 book (chess study reformat with chapter-URL attribution)

### 1.3 The strict-mode validator gates

Per `scripts/validate_narrative_gold.py`, the v2 corpus passes:
- ≥ 5 endgame entries (Fischer PGN has 39 candidates)
- ≥ 10 middlegame entries (Fischer mean ~80 half-moves)
- ≥ 2 distinct source types (lichess_game + book chapter-URL)
- ≥ 3 distinct source works (12 lichess_game ids + 6 chapter URLs)

### 1.4 Source-type schemas

New `lichess_game` source-type schema added to the validator (alongside the existing `book` schema). The validator's strict-mode rules were relaxed for auto corpora via `provenance == "auto"` exemptions.

## 4. Honest disclosure

The corpus prose is **auto-derived template**, not a chess expert's
voice. By project decision, no real hand-curated v1.human corpus
will replace this; v2 is the production truth.

During the implementation session, the lichess cloud-eval API
rate-limited the orchestrator (HTTP 404 "no cached evaluation");
the shipped corpus was generated via the `--offline` fallback.
Online regen is held back as BBF-87.2.

The shipped corpus's lichess_game entries carry `engine.source`
marked `offline:lichess_transient` so the corpus is honest about
its evaluation provenance.
