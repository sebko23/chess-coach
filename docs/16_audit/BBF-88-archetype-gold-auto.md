## 0. The problem at hand

The v1 archetype corpus (`tests/gold/archetypes/v1/corpus.json`, 14
placeholder entries) was a stub. The plan was to hand-curate a
v1.human corpus from chess experts, but the work was open-ended
and unscheduled.

The honest options were:
1. Wait for hand-curation (could be months).
2. Replace with a deterministic auto-derived v0 corpus that has a
   mechanical contract.

BBF-88 chose option 2, similar to BBF-87's approach for narrative_gold.

## 4. Honest disclosures

- **The v0 corpus covers 4 of 7 archetypes** (Tactician, Wildcard,
  Specialist not yet labeled). The kNN bootstrap produced whatever
  metric-vector cluster the input data fell into; the other 3
  archetypes require a different metric-vector shape that the
  synthetic fixture doesn't produce.

  not validated against real chess domain knowledge.
- **The 8-entry player pool includes real Lichess usernames**
  (ebassti, maia9) and real GM players (Fischer, Reshevsky, Tal,
  Gligoric, Geller).
